A team is a group of people sharing topics together. Everyone on the team reads the same feed, edits
the same topics, and talks in a group chat with Carl on the team's topics. The Coffee Talk panel travels with you. Its **…** menu lists every one of your
chat rooms from any page, and it stays on the one you picked as you move around. 

![The Teams page: your teams with their roles, members, topics, monthly spend, and a chat
badge on a team with unread mentions](../../../assets/screenshots/teams-your-teams.png)

## Create a team

Three ways in:

1. **New Team** on the **Teams** page.
2. **Team Up** on a topic page. The topic comes along, already added.
3. **New team…** in the **Team** field when you create or edit a topic. The topic gets added the
same way.

All three methods open the Team form: a name, an optional description, the topics the team starts with, and
invitations. Team names are unique across CarlNotes, and the form tells you if a
name is already taken. Use the topic list to find and add new topics to your team.

![The New team form: name, description, one topic picked, and the Members fields with the Public
toggle below them](../../../assets/screenshots/teams-new-team-modal.png)

Invitations entered while you create the team are sent the moment it exists. The **Public** toggle
determines whether the team has a public page.

## Add a topic to a team

**Team Up** sits on every topic or profile page. It lists the teams you lead. Pick one to add a topic or team member. 
An invited team member will see the invitation on their profile page. It will be up to them to accept by setting themself to **Active**.

![The Team Up menu on a topic page, listing the viewer's teams and New
team](../../../assets/screenshots/teams-team-up-menu.png)

![Your teams on a profile page: each team's role and who invited you, an Active toggle per row, and
an ✕ to leave](../../../assets/screenshots/profile-your-teams-active.png)

## Team Roles: leader and member

Every team has at least one leader. Members read, edit topics, and talk in the group chat. Leaders 
accept join requests, add and remove topics, change the team's name and
description, and can toggle the page public.

## The team page

Every team has a page that lists its members and its topics. Leaders can edit their team on this page.

![The Sunday Book Club team page: members with roles, the team's topics, and mention badges on
the title, a topic, and the Coffee Talk pill](../../../assets/screenshots/teams-team-page.png)

## Ask to join

On a team page you're not a member of, a **Join** button asks to join. Open that team's room in the
Coffee Talk panel and you get the same offer: "Join this team to start the conversation". Once you ask to join,
team's leaders see you in the members table with the **Active** toggle off; switching it on lets you
in. Use the **Join** button to add or remove your request to join a team.

![The Join Team button on a team page you're not on, showing a plus and the tooltip "Ask to join
Kickin it"](../../../assets/screenshots/teams-join-request.png)

Once you've asked, the same button takes the request back.

![The same button after asking, showing a minus and the tooltip "Delete request to join Kickin
it"](../../../assets/screenshots/teams-join-requested.png)

## Invitations

Three ways to invite:

- **By email.** The address gets an invitation email with a link.
- **By @username.** No email is sent. The invitation waits on their **Teams** page.
- **Invite by link.** Anyone with the link can join.

A daily allowance limits email and link invitations; @username invitations don't spend it. Whether
someone can be invited by @username is set in their account settings: anyone, only
people they interact with, or nobody.

![The Invitations setting on the account page: who may invite you, set to anyone, people I interact
with, or nobody](../../../assets/screenshots/account-invite-permission.png)

## What joining costs you

Nothing. Joining a team subscribes you to its topics. They appear in your feed, with email
updates off unless you turn them on. The topic's owner keeps paying for the brews.
