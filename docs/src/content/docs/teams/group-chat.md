Every topic a team has added gets its own shared Coffee Talk: multiple conversations per team, any member of
the team can join in, Carl at the table. Carl knows about the topic's findings, sources, and brew
history, just like he does in a [private Coffee Talk](/docs/feed/chat/).

## One panel, every page

There is one Coffee Talk, docked bottom-right on every page. It keeps the conversation you opened,
at the size you left it, as you move around the app. The panel doesn't belong to the page, the panel belongs to the conversation.

Each team's chat room is its own conversation, and what one team says about a topic does not show in
another team's room. A topic in more than one of your teams shows up in the menu for each of them. The avatar in front of the chat room is
what tells the rooms apart. Hover over the avatar to see the team's full name.

## Who hears you

The name above the message box shows who your message goes to. A message defaults to
**@all** and every member is notified, including Carl who will answer. To reply to one person instead, type their
@username, or use the menu to select them. You can reply to a previous message by clicking on
**Reply** below that message.

When someone mentions you or replies to one of your messages, a count appears everywhere that
conversation is reachable. Opening its chat room clears the mention count badge.

## Sharing files

The paperclip attaches a file to your next message: an image, a PDF, or a text file. Unlike a
private Coffee Talk, a file shared here belongs to the room:

- **Every member can download it** from the chip under the message.
- **Carl reads it** and can quote it in his answers, for everyone.
- **You can delete it**, and so can a team leader. Deleting removes it from Carl's future answers.

Each team member can have up to 20 files shared per room. Don't share a file with the room unless you
want the whole team (and Carl) to read it.

## What stays private

Your private Coffee Talks with Carl stay private. Nothing you attached or asked in a one-on-one conversation
with Carl follows you into a chat room, even on the same topic. The team only knows what was said and
shared in the chat room.

![A private Coffee Talk on a topic no team has added: one cup in the header, a question and Carl's
answer, nobody else in the room](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-private-room.png)

One cup means you've got Carl to yourself.
