Coffee Talk is the chat panel docked to the bottom-right corner of every page. One panel rides in
the app shell, so the conversation you have open stays open, as you move
around the app. On a topic one of your teams holds, opening the panel starts you in that team's
shared chat room, and the panel's **…** menu lists the other rooms you can join, as well as your private
chat on this topic (see [Group chat with Carl](/docs/teams/group-chat/)). What makes it different is that Carl answers anyone from what the open conversation is about: a topic's
findings, or every topic a team holds when you are in the team's chat room. His answers are anchored to what he actually read for you. He
can also run a web search mid-conversation if your question needs it, and the search bills to your
CarlNotes account's Coffee talk budget.

![A private Coffee Talk: the question above Carl's answer, each behind its author's avatar and name,
drawn from the topic's own findings](../../../assets/screenshots/feed-coffee-talk.png)

## Questions worth asking

Not categories. These start the conversation about your topic:

- "What’s changed since last month?"
- "Where do two of my sources disagree?"
- "Summarize everything about pricing in one paragraph."
- "Which three of the last twenty findings matter most for someone shipping next week?"
- "What am I not seeing? What's missing from this feed?"

That last one doubles as a source audit: gaps Carl names are gaps in your source list or prompt.

## Bringing a file into the conversation

Three ways, all on the message box:

1. **The paperclip** opens a file picker.
2. **Drag a file onto the message box.** It becomes a drop zone reading "Drop to attach".
3. **Paste an image** from the clipboard.

![The message box while a file is dragged over it, showing the Drop to attach
zone](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-drop-to-attach.png)

An attached file appears as a chip above the box, with an X to remove it.

![An attached file shown as a chip above the message box, with an X to remove
it](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-attached-file-chip.png)

A file brought into a conversation is read for the conversation only. It never becomes a topic
attachment, and it never changes how future brews are scored. Your private chat on a topic is yours
alone, and chat text is encrypted at rest either way. A team room is not private: everyone on the
team reads what you say there and can open what you attach. Context that should describe how a topic
is brewed belongs in [Attachments](/docs/topics/attachments/).

## What Carl won't do here

Carl won't create topics, change prompts, add sources, or run brews from inside a conversation. The
boundary is deliberate. A conversation is a place to brainstorm. Carl won't
spend your budget because a sentence sounded like an instruction. When a conversation convinces you
to change the topic, you can change it in the topic's editor and kick off a brew.

## Getting better answers

- **Name the time window.** "Since Tuesday" beats "recently".
- **Ask for the disagreement, not the consensus.** The consensus is already there in the feed.
- **Ask what's missing.** It's the quickest way to identify gaps in your source list and prompt.
