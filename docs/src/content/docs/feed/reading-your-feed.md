Your topic feed is every topic's findings in one place: the homepage lists your topics with their
newest findings, and each topic page holds the full historical set.

![A topic card in the feed: ranked findings, each with its source and
age](../../../assets/screenshots/feed-cards.png)

## Read the finding before you open the link

Two ways the topic feed can save you time.

**The resource kind label signals time commitment.** Every finding is marked read, watch, or listen. The
label exists so you can tell a two-minute article from a forty-minute video before you decide to open it.
Filter by resource kind from the dropdown in the search bar.

**Read Carl's note.** The headline says what the topic finding is. **Carl's Notes**, in
the finding's popover, says why it *matters*.

## Rate, mark read, filter

Open a finding's popover for its controls:

- **Rate this find**: thumbs up or thumbs down.
- **Mark read**: your feed keeps track of the things you haven't read yet. The **All**
  view mutes what you've already read, and the **Unread** view hides it entirely. Bookmark anything you'll
  want to keep at the top.

![The finding popover's controls: Bookmark, Mark read, and Rate this
find](../../../assets/screenshots/feed-thumbs-and-consumed.png)

Sort the feed by **Relevance**, **Newest**, or **Trending**. Filter by resource kind, by tags, or by view
with the **Filters** control. The **Reheat** button reloads the topic feed in place.

## Email updates

Each topic you own can email you its new findings after a brew. The toggle lives on the **Activity**
page, in the **Emails** column of your topics table. Subscribed topics have the same toggle in their
own table.

Turning the toggle off stops the email from being sent. But the topic keeps brewing and its findings stay up to date 
in the feed. 

The unsubscribe link inside the email deactivates your subscription to another user's topic. 
Nothing is deleted, and the email or subscription can be reactivated on the Activity page.

## The Brew diary is your topic brew's history

Every topic page has a **Brew diary**: one summary per brew, with "read N · kept N" counts and that
brew's recap. It's the fastest way to answer one question: did the prompt rewrite help? Rewrite,
press **Brew**, and compare the new entry's kept count and findings against the entries before it.
A diary entry tells you right away if your prompt is working or not.

## When you want a discussion

The topic feed gives you individual findings. When the question is "what changed this month" or "where do
these sources disagree", ask [Coffee Talk](/docs/feed/chat/). Chat with Carl about what your topic brew found.
