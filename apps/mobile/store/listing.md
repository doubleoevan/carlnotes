## Description (Apple and Play, 2,874 of 4,000)

Carl is a raccoon. He drinks coffee and reads everything. You give him a topic you care about, and he reads its
sources on a schedule, keeps what matches your prompt, and writes a note on why each finding matters. Then he puts a
pot on and does it again. CarlNotes is where you read what he kept.

The app signs in with the same account as carlnotes.com. Your topics, your teams, and your chats are the same ones.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Read your feed. Your topics, the topics you follow, and the featured and popular ones, each with its newest
findings ranked by relevance. Sort by newest or trending, filter to unread or bookmarked, and open any finding's link.

• Make and follow topics. Create a topic with a title, a prompt in your own words, and sources, right from the app.
Follow a public topic to get its findings in your feed. Each topic page shows its findings and a recap of what Carl
read and kept on the last scan.

• Listen to Coffee Break. After a scan, Carl and Vienna talk the findings through in a short podcast episode. Play it
in the app, skip by chapter, read the transcript, and keep listening with the screen off. The player stays with you
from screen to screen.

• Talk to Carl. Coffee Talk is a private chat with Carl about your topics. Ask him about what he kept, and let him
edit a topic with you. He proposes the change, you accept it.

• Talk with your team. Teams share topics and a chat room. Reply to a message, mention someone, and pick up a
thread where you left it.

• Write Tasting Notes. A note is a page you and your team keep on a topic, with formatting, links, and comments.
Drafts you write offline wait on the device until you reconnect.

• Search. Topics, podcast episodes, findings, teams, and people from one box.

• Keep up. Activity lists the topics you follow and your invitations. The badge on the app icon counts what is new.

• Open links in the app. Once installed, a link to a topic, a note, a podcast episode, a team, or an invitation on
carlnotes.com opens here.

• Light and dark. The app follows your device, or you pick one.

SIGN IN

Continue with Apple, Google, GitHub, or email. One account across the website and the app.

REPORT AND BLOCK

Chat rooms, notes, and comments are written by people. Report a message, a note, or a comment from its own menu, and
block a person from the same sheet. Blocked people disappear from your rooms, notes, and comments.

ABOUT PLANS

Plans is a read-only page in this release. It shows the plans and marks yours. The app sells nothing. Carl runs on a
monthly model budget per account. He calls it coffee. Account shows how much of the month's coffee is left.

CarlNotes is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. The privacy policy is at carlnotes.com/privacy. Carl does not
sell your data, serve ads, or train our own models on your content.
