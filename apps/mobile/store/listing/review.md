# Store review answers

The answers both stores ask for beside the listing copy: the categories, the age rating questionnaires, the URLs, and
the review notes template. The copy itself is in `app-store.md` and `google-play.md` in this folder.

## Categories

- Apple: primary News, secondary Productivity.
- Play: News & Magazines.

## Age rating

Apple's questionnaire: None for every content item (cartoon or fantasy violence, realistic violence, sexual content,
profanity, horror, alcohol, gambling, medical, mature themes, contests). Unrestricted web access: No. The app opens
carlnotes.com pages, the docs, and a YouTube player, and the player's web view is limited to the player document and
YouTube's embed frames. Gambling: No. The app has user generated content: chat rooms, notes, and comments, with
report and block on each one, a moderation queue in the admin console, and versioned Terms every account accepts.
Result: set by the store from these answers. Record it here after submission.

Google Play's questionnaire: No to violence, sexuality, language, controlled substances, and gambling. Users interact:
Yes, in chat rooms, notes, and comments. Users share personal information: Yes, what they type in those places. Users
share location: No. Digital purchases: Yes. Result: set by the store from these answers. Record it here after
submission.

Play's User Generated Content policy: in-app reporting and blocking exist on messages, notes, and comments. Reports
reach the admin console. The Terms of Service cover conduct.

## Review notes

The template below is pasted into App Review's notes and Play's app access instructions. The two placeholder values
live in Doppler, so paste them by hand into the submission and never into this file.

```
CarlNotes is a reading app for a research service at carlnotes.com. The free plan is enough to review every screen.
Plus and Premium are auto-renewing subscriptions, monthly or yearly, sold through In-App Purchase on iOS and Google
Play Billing on Android. Plans shows each plan's store price and period, the renewal line, and the Terms of Service
and Privacy Policy links under it. A purchase in review runs in the store's sandbox and charges nothing.

Review account
  Email: <REVIEWER_EMAIL>
  Password: <REVIEWER_PASSWORD>
This account has seeded content: the Topic "Carl's own notes", the Team "Review crew", and a followed public Topic with
a published Podcast Episode.

Steps
1. Sign in: open the app, choose Continue with email, and enter the account above.
2. On Home, open the Topic "Carl's own notes" from Your topics.
3. Read its Findings. Each one has Carl's note and a link. Open the scan recap under the heading.
4. Open the Note on the Topic page, under Notes. The editor opens for this account.
5. Open the menu, choose Teams, open the Team "Review crew", and open its chat room from the Team page.
6. Back on Home, tap "All of it." in the headline to open the Topics tab. Open the followed public Topic under Your
   followed topics and play its Podcast Episode. Playback continues after leaving the screen and with the screen
   locked.
7. Open the menu, choose Account. The page shows the profile fields, the plan, the budget, Restore purchases,
   Blocked people, and Close your account.
8. Buy a plan: open the menu, choose Plans, turn on Bill yearly for the yearly price, and tap Upgrade on Plus.
   Confirm in the store's sheet with a sandbox account. Account shows the Plus plan, and its Manage plan button opens
   the store's subscription settings, where the plan is canceled.
9. Restore purchases: Account > Restore purchases. The alert names the restored plan.
10. Report a note: open the followed public Topic's page and its Notes, which its owner wrote. Open a note's menu and
    choose Report note. The sheet sends the report and offers Block for the author.
11. Block a person: open the Topic owner's profile from the Topic page, open its menu, and choose Block. The person
    leaves your rooms, notes, and comments. Account > Blocked people lists them, with Unblock.
12. Delete the account: Account > Close your account > Close account, then confirm. The session ends and the account
    and its content are removed. With a store plan, the section says the store keeps billing until the plan is
    canceled there, beside a button to the store's subscription settings.

Permissions
  Photos and Camera: asked only when the reviewer attaches a photo in a chat or a topic, or picks an avatar.
  Microphone: asked only when the reviewer taps the mic to ask Carl a question out loud.
  Notifications: alerts for a topic scan's new findings and chat mentions, each with its own switch in Account, and
  the badge count on the icon. None is needed for the steps above.
  Background audio: Podcast Episode playback.

Sign in with Apple is offered beside Google, GitHub, and email. The account above uses email.
Apple App Attest and Google Play Integrity check that an email sign-up comes from a genuine install.
```
