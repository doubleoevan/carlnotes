# Store description

The long description both stores share, ready to paste into the App Store's Description and Google Play's Full
description (3,886 of 4,000, counted with each paragraph on one line). The lines below wrap at 120, so join each
paragraph's lines into one when pasting. Every feature named here exists in the app: the page inventory in
`../../parity.md` and the sections of `../../AGENTS.md` are the source. The short fields are in `app-store.md` and
`google-play.md`.

```text
Carl is a raccoon. He drinks coffee and reads everything. You give him a topic you care about, and he reads its
sources on a schedule, keeps what matches your prompt, and writes a note on why each finding matters. Then he puts a
pot on and does it again. CarlNotes is where you read what he kept.

The app signs in with the same account as carlnotes.com. Your topics, your teams, and your chats are the same ones.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Read your feed. Your topics, the topics you follow, and the popular ones, each with its newest findings ranked by
relevance. Sort by newest or trending, filter to unread or bookmarked, and open any finding's link.

• Make and follow topics. Create a topic with a title, a prompt in your own words, and sources, right from the app.
Follow a public topic to get its findings in your feed. Each topic page shows its findings and a recap of what Carl
read and kept on the last scan.

• Listen to Coffee Break. After a scan, Carl and Vienna talk the findings through in a short podcast episode. Play it
in the app, skip by chapter, read the transcript, and keep listening with the screen off. The player stays with you
from screen to screen.

• Watch Coffee Break Cinema. Many episodes come with a film: the same conversation with the pages and images Carl
read on screen, chapter by chapter.

• Talk to Carl. Coffee Talk is a private chat with Carl about your topics. Type a question or ask it out loud, and
attach a photo, a camera shot, or a file. Ask him about what he kept, and let him edit a topic with you. He proposes
the change, you accept it.

• Talk with your team. Teams share topics and a chat room. Reply to a message, mention someone, and pick up a thread
where you left it.

• Write Tasting Notes. A note is a page you and your team keep on a topic, with formatting, links, and comments.
Drafts you write offline wait on the device until you reconnect.

• Post and react. Write a post on a topic, leave a hot take for Carl and Vienna, and react to posts and comments.

• Keep up. Notifications tell you when a topic's scan finds something new or someone mentions you, and you pick
which ones you get. The News lists what changed on your topics and teams, and the badge on the app icon counts what
is new.

• See the Specials. Each team and profile has a board of what its topics read and kept, with the day's numbers.

• Keep a Brew diary. Carl remembers what each topic has been about, and the diary shows it day by day.

• Search. Topics, podcast episodes, findings, teams, and people from one box.

• Open links in the app. Once installed, a link to a topic, a note, a podcast episode, a team, or an invite on
carlnotes.com opens here.

• Light and dark. The app follows your device, or you pick one.

SIGN IN

Continue with Apple, Google, GitHub, or email. One account across the website and the app.

REPORT AND BLOCK

Chat rooms, notes, posts, and comments are written by people. Report any of them from its own menu, and block a
person from the same sheet. Blocked people disappear from your rooms, notes, and comments.

ABOUT PLANS

CarlNotes is free to use. Plus and Premium are auto-renewing subscriptions, monthly or yearly, bought in the app
through your App Store or Google Play account. They raise the topic, scan, and invite limits and the month's budget.
Each plan shows the store's price. A plan renews until you cancel it in the store's subscription settings, and Restore
purchases in Account returns a plan bought on another phone. Carl runs on a monthly model budget per account. He calls
it coffee. Account shows how much of the month's coffee is left.

CarlNotes is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. The privacy policy is at carlnotes.com/privacy, and the terms of
use are at carlnotes.com/terms. Carl does not sell your data, serve ads, or train our own models on your content.

Carl stays up. You stay informed.
```
