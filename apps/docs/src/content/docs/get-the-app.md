CarlNotes is an app for iPhone, iPad, and Android. It signs in with the same account as carlnotes.com, and your topics,
teams, and chats are the same ones.

- App Store: the link goes here once the listing is published
- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.carlnotes.app)

## What the app does

The app covers reading and talking. Voice questions, the new-topic chat, chat attachments, and the admin console stay on
the site. You read your feed and each topic's findings and recaps, make a topic, and follow a public one. You talk to
Carl in Coffee Talk and to your team in its room. You write Tasting Notes and comments, with offline drafts that wait
until you reconnect. You play Coffee Break episodes and keep listening with the screen off. Report and block work the
same way as on the site.

Make a topic as [the Quickstart](/docs/quickstart/) describes. Plans is read-only in the app: it shows the plans and
marks yours, and changing a plan happens on the site.

## Links open in the app

Once the app is installed, a link to carlnotes.com opens in it: a topic, a note, a Coffee Break episode, a team, a
profile, an invitation, a search, Activity, and your account. A link to the docs, the Terms, or the Privacy Policy stays
in your browser.

## When Carl asks for a newer version

If the app is older than the one the service expects, Carl says so and the app waits. Press **Update CarlNotes**, which
opens the store page, update there, and come back. **Check again** picks up where you left off. The **Account** screen
names the version, build, and update you have, for a support request.
