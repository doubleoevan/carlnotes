The CarlNotes app for iPhone and Android can notify you about two things: new findings on a topic you follow, and
someone mentioning you in a team chat. It sends nothing else, and the website sends no notifications.

## What a notification says

A topic notification comes after a scheduled scan keeps new findings. It names the topic, counts the new findings, and
quotes the first one, like "2 new findings, including “A cheaper grinder burr”". A scan that keeps nothing new sends
no notification, though its email still says so. A scan you run yourself sends no notification either.

A chat notification names the room and the person, like "alex mentioned you" or "alex replied to you". It never shows
the message itself. Someone you blocked, or a message you reported, sends you nothing, and you are never notified
about your own messages. A message to @all notifies nobody.

Tap a notification to open its topic or its chat room.

## When the app asks

The app doesn't ask at launch. The first time you follow a topic or open a team chat, it explains the notifications,
and the system asks only if you choose **Turn on notifications**. **Not now** closes the explanation for good on that
phone. You can turn notifications on later from Account.

## The switches in Account

Account has a **Notifications** section with one switch for topic findings and one for team chat mentions. Both turn on
the first time you allow notifications. A switch you turn off stays off on every phone you sign in on.

A topic notifies you only while you follow it with its emails on. Unfollowing a topic, or turning its emails off, stops
both its emails and its notifications.

If you refused notifications, the section says that notifications are off in your phone's settings and opens them for
you. On iPhone, that's **Settings**, **Notifications**, **CarlNotes**. On Android, it's **Settings**, **Apps**,
**CarlNotes**, **Notifications**, where each kind also has its own channel, named like its switch.

## Signing out

Signing out stops your notifications on that phone. If someone else signs in on the same phone, they get only their own
notifications.
