CarlNotes plans are built around brew volume. Every brew fetches pages and runs AI models, and both cost money. The
plans track the parts that cost: how many topics you have, how often they brew, and how many brews you start by hand in
a day.

## The plans

| | Free | Plus | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price, monthly billing | $0 | $20/month | $40/month |
| Price, yearly billing | $0 | $200/year | $400/year |
| Topics | 3 | 10 | 25 |
| Topics on a daily schedule | 1 | 3 monthly · 4 yearly | 6 monthly · 7 yearly |
| Brews a day | 5 | 15 monthly · 20 yearly | 30 monthly · 40 yearly |
| Coffee fund a month | $3 | $15 | $35 |
| Members per team you lead | 15 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Coffee Break podcast episodes | Up to 10 minutes per brew, latest kept | Up to 30 minutes per brew, every full one kept | Up to 30 minutes per brew, every full one kept |

Yearly billing costs ten times the monthly price instead of twelve, so you get two months free. Yearly plans get higher
daily limits instead of overage. A yearly subscription has no monthly invoice to add overage to.

"Topics on a daily schedule" limits how many of your topics run **Daily** or **Weekdays**. Weekly topics don't count
against this limit. "Brews a day" counts scheduled and manual brews together. The count resets at midnight UTC.

## Buy a plan in the app

In the iPhone and Android app, **Plans** sells Plus and Premium through your App Store or Google Play account, monthly
or yearly. Each card shows the store's price in your store's currency, which can differ from the prices above. The plan
renews until you cancel it in the store. A plan bought in the app has the same limits and coffee fund as one bought on
the site, and the site shows it too.

If you already pay on the site, the app's **Plans** and **Account** say "Your plan is billed on the web." instead of
offering a purchase. Manage that plan on the site's account page. If you bought in the app, the site's **Plans** and
account page show **Manage in the App Store** or **Manage in Google Play** instead of checkout, and the button opens
your store's subscription settings. If a plan from the app ranks above the one you pay for on the site, as after a
restore, the site's subscription is canceled at the end of the period you paid for, so you aren't billed for both.

**Restore purchases** on the app's **Account** screen brings back a plan your store account bought, after a reinstall or
on a new phone. A store account's plan belongs to one CarlNotes account at a time, so restoring it while signed in to
another account moves it there.

Where your store doesn't sell CarlNotes plans, **Plans** shows the prices without a purchase.

## Overage

On a monthly paid plan bought on the site with a card on file, reaching the daily brew limit doesn't stop you. Manual
brews past the limit keep working until the month's spend reaches 90% of the coffee fund. Each one bills as metered
overage on your monthly invoice. The account page says so: "Extra scans beyond your daily limit are billed by the scan."

On the free plan, on a plan bought in the app, or without a card, the daily limit stops brews until midnight UTC. Yearly
plans don't bill overage. Their higher daily limits replace it.

There is also a monthly limit. Your account page shows **Carl's coffee fund**: the month's AI model spend against your
plan's monthly limit: $3 on **Free**, $15 on **Plus**, or $35 on **Premium**. The plans page shows each fund, and the
brews it buys as an estimate: "About 30, 150, or 350 Brews a month". If the coffee fund runs out, your brews pause until
it resets on the first of the month, UTC.

## When the coffee fund runs low

Features pause until the next month as Carl runs out of coffee. As the month's spend climbs, they pause in this order,
and brews pause last:

| | Free | Plus | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speaking with Carl and Vienna pauses. You can still text with Carl | 15% | 40% | 50% |
| New podcast episodes go short and replace the last short one | Always short | 60% | 70% |
| New podcast episodes pause | 20% | 75% | 85% |
| Daily brews go weekly | Moves each day | Moves each day | Moves each day |
| Manual brews pause. Scheduled brews keep running | 90% | 90% | 90% |
| Every brew pauses until the 1st | 100% | 100% | 100% |

![The plans page's Feature windows table: each cutoff's share of the coffee fund on Free, Plus, and
Premium](../../../assets/screenshots/plans-feature-windows.png)

The daily brews cutoff moves. It sits where the rest of the fund covers your daily brews to the 1st, so it starts low
and climbs each day. Past it, each daily topic brews once a week. Manual brews keep working until 90%.

A short podcast episode covers up to 5 findings instead of 15. Past the speaking cutoff, the microphone opens the plans
page. On **Premium**, it says when speaking comes back instead.

The [plans page](https://carlnotes.com/plans) shows the same table under **Feature windows**. The account page marks
each cutoff on the coffee fund's bar with a circle. Hover a circle to see what it pauses. A list under the bar says the
same, and strikes through each cutoff the month has passed.

## Track your budget

Go to your own **Profile** page. Your topics table has a **Cost** column with each topic's total for the month. Expand a
topic to see the brews that charged it. The last row shows the month's total. The **Cost** column tells you which topics
spent the most.

## Upgrade, downgrade, cancel

**Manage plan** on the account page opens checkout for upgrades and manages the subscription you have. Canceling puts
you back on the free plan's limits.

A plan bought in the app is switched on the app's **Plans** screen and canceled in your store's subscription settings,
which **Manage plan** in the app opens. A canceled store plan lasts to the end of the period you paid for. A refunded
one ends when the store refunds it.

Downgrading never deletes anything. Every topic and finding stays, and you keep following the topics you follow. Two
limits tighten:

- If you're over the topic limit, you can't create new topics until you're back under it. Your existing topics keep
  working.
- If you're over the new daily-schedule limit, your oldest daily topics keep their schedule. The newer ones stop brewing
  on their own. They keep their findings, and you can still brew them by hand. Switch them to a **Weekly** schedule, and
  they no longer count against the daily limit.

## Closing your account

**Close account** is at the bottom of the account page. The page states the terms: "Your topics, findings,
and chats go with it, and you stop following every topic. Any paid plan is canceled. This cannot be undone."

Closing your account can't cancel a plan bought in the app. It keeps billing through the App Store or Google Play until
you cancel it there, so the section says so and links to your store's subscription settings. Cancel it there before or
after you close the account. Closing deletes the record RevenueCat, the service that checks app purchases, keeps for
your account.
