CarlNotes is a shared room, and Carl keeps it civil. You can report anything another person wrote, block a person
so nothing of theirs reaches you, and every report is acted on within a day. Carl also declines a post that is
abusive before it reaches anyone.

## Report something

Every piece of writing by another person has a **Report** action:

- A chat message: the flag at the end of the message, beside Reply.
- A note comment: **Report comment** beside the comment.
- A note: **Report note** at the foot of the open note.
- A topic, a team, or a profile: **Report issue** in the page's actions menu, the **⋮** button in the header.

The report asks what is wrong, in your own words. For a message, a note, a comment, or a profile it also offers to
block the person in the same step. Once you send it, a reported message, note, or comment disappears for you right
away. Nobody else sees a
change until someone has looked.

A person reviews every report within a day and either removes the content or keeps it. Removed content is deleted the
same way its author would delete it. Either way the report is closed, and you are not asked to do anything more. If
you want to tell us more, write to [support@carlnotes.com](mailto:support@carlnotes.com).

## Block a person

Blocking someone keeps their messages, comments, notes, and invitations away from you. You stop seeing what they
write, and they can no longer invite you to a topic or a team. They are not told. A reply to one of their messages
shows a hidden quote in its place.

Block from a report, or from **Block** in a profile's actions menu. Your blocked people are listed on the Account page
under **Blocked people**, each with an **Unblock** control.

## What Carl declines to post

Before a message, a note, a comment, a username, a team, or a public topic is saved, Carl checks it for abuse: threats,
harassment, hate, sexual content involving minors, and the like. If it fails, Carl says so and keeps your draft, so you
can change it and send again. Nothing is reported and nothing is recorded. If the check itself cannot run, your post
goes through, and we are told the check failed.

## The Terms

Posting means accepting the current [Terms of Service](https://carlnotes.com/terms), which say what can be posted,
how reports work, and when content comes down. Creating an account accepts them. When the Terms change, a dialog asks
you to accept the new version the next time you open CarlNotes. You can put it off and keep reading, and the dialog
returns the next time you post.

Questions, or a report you would rather make by email: [support@carlnotes.com](mailto:support@carlnotes.com).
