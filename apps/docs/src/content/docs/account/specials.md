The Daily Specials Board shows who visits your topics, findings, and posts, and who plays your podcasts and films. Its
charts count by day and by place, and Carl writes a paragraph about the numbers once a day. The board has three pages,
each headed Daily Specials:

- **Topic Specials** counts the visits to a person's topics, findings, and Open Mic posts, across all of their teams
  and their topics on no team.
- **Podcast Specials** counts the plays of a person's podcast episodes and films, across all of their teams and their
  topics on no team.
- **Team Specials** counts the visits to a team's topics, findings, and posts.

A visit is one person opening a topic, a finding, or a post. It counts once per person, item, and day, signed in or
not. A play is one start of a podcast episode or a film, finished or not. A podcast app that retries a download counts
once.

## Open your Specials

Open the user menu and choose **Topic Specials**, **Podcast Specials**, or **Team Specials**. In the CarlNotes app, the
same three rows are on the **Account** screen. **Team Specials** opens the first of your teams in alphabetical order, or
the **Teams** page if you're on no team. On Team Specials, the team menu at the top right opens your other teams.

Anyone's Specials open from their pages too. The **Visitors** line at the bottom of a Café links to that person's Topic
Specials and Podcast Specials, and a team page's Visitors line links to its Team Specials.

## What the page shows

The top of the page shows the person's or team's picture and name over their totals:

- **Topic Specials**: topics, findings, posts, and visitors.
- **Podcast Specials**: podcasts, podcast episodes, podcast plays, and film plays.
- **Team Specials**: topics, findings, posts, members, and visitors.

The visitors and plays in the totals cover the last 365 days, like the charts below them.

Five sections follow, all open. Carl's paragraph opens the first one, **Popular**, under its menu and above its chart.
He writes it in his own hand from the numbers alone: the totals, the last 30 days against the 30 before, and the
countries on the map. He never reads a title or a hot take to write it.

The five sections:

- **Popular 🔥**: the ten items with the most visits or plays, most first.
- **Slices**: each item's share of the visits or plays.
- **Poetry**: words from the items' titles, larger for higher counts. A word counts times, not people. One person can
  read several of the items whose titles hold it.
- **Performance**: each item's visits or plays by day.
- **Visitors**: a map of where the visits or plays came from.

A chart with nothing to count yet says **No visitors yet.** or **No plays yet.**, and the map shows no pins.

## Choose what a chart counts

On Topic Specials and Team Specials, each chart has its own menu of **Topics**, **Findings**, and **Posts**, starting on
Topics. With Findings, Slices and Performance show the top ten findings and put the rest in **Other**.

On Podcast Specials, each chart has its own **Podcasts**, **Films**, and **Both** buttons, starting on Both. Films
counts film plays, Podcasts counts the other plays, and Both counts all of them. Under Both, each count splits into
podcast plays and film plays.

Performance has its own range: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days**, or **365 days**, starting on 30 days. Each point
is a day, except on 365 days, where each point is a week. Every page opens on these choices, so a choice you make on
one page never changes another.

On the website, hover over a bar, a slice, a word, or a day for its details. Click a bar or a slice to open its item.
Click a day in Performance to keep its details open, then click a line there to open its item. In the CarlNotes app,
tap instead: the details open in a sheet with a button to the item's page.

## The map

A city gets its own pin once ten different people visited, or played, an item from there. Smaller places count toward
their country, whose pin sits at the country's center. Pins close together share one pin with their total. Click or tap
a shared pin to zoom in to its places. Click or tap one place's pin to list the 20 items visited or played most there,
each with how many times.

## Who sees what

On your own Specials, you see everything you own, private topics included. A team's members see everything on the
team's topics they can open. Everyone else sees public topics, findings, posts, podcasts, and films only. Someone who
isn't on a team sees only the totals on its Team Specials, with no paragraph and no charts.

Making a topic private takes it off your public Specials at once.

## When the numbers change

The page changes once a day, after midnight UTC, and counts through the day before. Everything but Performance
covers the last 365 days. Counting started the day Specials launched, so earlier visits and plays don't show.

## Share your Specials

Choose **Share** in the page's actions menu to share its link. A Topic Specials or Podcast Specials link previews with
the page's Popular chart. A Team Specials link previews with the team's own image.

The first topic email you get each month also has a line that links to your Topic Specials.

## Ask your AI

With CarlNotes in your AI app, say "how are my topics doing", "how is my podcast doing", or "how is my team doing".
Your AI reads the totals and Carl's paragraph and links to the page. See [Add to AI](/docs/account/add-to-ai/).

## Privacy

Specials stores no IP address and no single visit or play. A place is the country, region, and city that Cloudflare
reports for a visit or a play, and each day's counts are kept per item and place for 400 days. The
[privacy policy](/privacy) has the details.
