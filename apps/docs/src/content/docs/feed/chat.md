On the web, Coffee Talk is the chat panel docked to the bottom-right corner of every page. The panel lives in the app shell, so the
conversation you have open stays open as you move around the app. On a topic one of your teams holds, the panel opens in
that team's shared chat room. The panel's **…** menu lists the other rooms you can join and your private chat on this
topic (see [Group chat with Carl](/docs/teams/group-chat/)). Carl answers anyone from what the open conversation is
about. That is a topic's findings, or every topic a team holds in that team's chat room. His answers come from the pages
he read for you. If your question needs it, he can also run a web search during the conversation. The search counts
against the coffee fund on your CarlNotes account.

## Coffee Talk on your phone

Tap the orange **Coffee Talk** pill at the bottom right of any page. A topic held by one of your teams opens that
team's room. Tap the selector above the conversation to open a full-width sheet. It lists **Private chat** first,
then rooms alphabetically, with Team avatars and an orange scroll indicator. On Profile pages, the same shared-Team
and Topic-room preferences as web choose the conversation. Other pages open the web default room, or the new-topic chat
if you have no rooms. Without rooms, you can also pick a topic from your Feed for private chat.
The conversation fills the phone screen. **Close** returns to the page and reading position you came from. The pill
sits above the phone's safe area and gets out of the way while you type.

Type above the keyboard and tap **Send**. Carl's private answer appears as it arrives; **Stop** interrupts it. Copy an
answer with its copy button. The **…** menu offers **Clear private chat** behind a confirmation. Returning to a
conversation reloads its saved history. A connection interruption never sends your question a second time automatically.

The phone uses the same conversation, permissions, Topic proposals, and rejection wording as the web. Tables in answers
become cards with a label for each column. Raw HTML remains text, and only HTTP and HTTPS links open. Files already in
history appear as name chips. The selector also lists **Give Carl a topic. You know the one.**, the
[new-topic chat](/docs/feed/making-a-topic-in-chat/). To speak with Carl or hear an answer, use the web.

### Files on your phone

The paperclip beside the message box offers **Photo library**, **Take photo**, and **Files**. The app asks for the
camera only when you choose **Take photo**, and the app sees only the photos you pick from the library. A large photo is
made smaller before Carl reads it, and its location data is removed. The file types and limits are the same as on the
web. Each file shows as a chip above the box with an **X** to remove it.

On a topic, the files Carl keeps for that chat sit behind the file button with an **X** next to the paperclip. Tap a
file to open it, or its **X** to delete it and free a place for another.

## Questions worth asking

These questions start a conversation about your topic:

- "What’s changed since last month?"
- "Where do two of my sources disagree?"
- "Summarize everything about pricing in one paragraph."
- "Which three of the last twenty findings matter most for someone shipping next week?"
- "What am I not seeing? What's missing from this feed?"

That last one doubles as a source audit. The gaps Carl names are gaps in your source list or prompt.

## Bringing a file into the conversation

On the web there are three ways, all on the message box:

1. **The paperclip** opens a file picker.
2. **Drag a file onto the message box.** The box turns into a drop zone that reads "Drop to attach".
3. **Paste an image** from the clipboard.

![The message box while a file is dragged over it, showing the Drop to attach
zone](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-drop-to-attach.png)

An attached file appears as a chip above the box, with an X to remove it.

![An attached file shown as a chip above the message box, with an X to remove
it](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-attached-file-chip.png)

If Carl can't read a file, the message doesn't send, and he says so. A file too large for him to read in about a minute
and a half says that instead, so try a smaller one or just the pages you need. If he is busy reading other files, he
asks you to send it again in a moment.

Carl reads a file you bring into a conversation for that conversation only. It never becomes a topic attachment, and it
never changes how future brews are scored. Your private chat on a topic is yours alone. Chat text is stored encrypted,
in private chats and team rooms alike. A team room is not private. Everyone on the team reads what you say there and can
open what you attach. Context that should describe how a topic is brewed belongs in
[Attachments](/docs/topics/attachments/).

## Talk to Carl

On the web, while the message box is empty, the send button is a microphone. Tap it and ask. Carl listens while you talk. He sends
the question on his own 1.5 seconds after you stop, so there is nothing to press when you are done. A bar takes the
box's place, with a cancel button, a level meter, a timer, and a send button for sending early. A question sends itself
after one minute. If Carl hears no words, he says he didn't catch that, and the box comes back empty.

![Coffee Talk listening to a question: the bar in place of the message box shows the cancel button, the level meter, the
timer, and the send button](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-talk-to-carl-recording.png)

Carl answers out loud, in his podcast voice. He starts talking once his first sentence or two are written, while the
rest is still arriving. His answer is on the screen too, under the question you asked. Every answer of his has a play
button, whether you typed or spoke. Press it to hear any reply from the start, and press it again to stop. If a podcast
episode is playing, it pauses while you talk and while Carl answers.

![Carl's answer in Coffee Talk, with the play button under it beside the copy button, and the microphone in the empty
message box](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-talk-to-carl-play.png)

One tap starts a whole conversation. Once Carl finishes talking, the microphone opens again on its own, so you can keep
going without touching the screen. While he talks, a ring around the microphone shows that it will open for your next
question. Say nothing for 8 seconds and the conversation ends quietly, or tap cancel on the bar. Stopping Carl
mid-answer or typing a question also ends it. The next tap starts a new conversation.

Speaking with Carl pauses once the month's spend reaches 15% of the coffee fund on **Free**, 40% on **Plus**, or 50% on
**Premium**. Past that, the microphone opens the plans page. On **Premium**, it says when speaking comes back instead.
You can still text with Carl. See
[When the coffee fund runs low](/docs/account/plans-and-limits/#when-the-coffee-fund-runs-low).

A question asked from the podcast player's microphone shows up here too. It sits under a small tag naming the episode
and the chapter it was asked during. Tap the tag to play that chapter. If Vienna answered it, her answer shows her
picture and her name, and its play button reads it in her voice. Every question you ask here is Carl's to answer. See
[Coffee Break podcast](/docs/feed/coffee-break/#talk-with-carl-and-vienna).

![A question asked from the podcast player in the topic's private chat, tagged with its episode and chapter, and
Vienna's answer under her picture and name](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-podcast-question.png)

CarlNotes never keeps a recording. Google turns the recording into text with storage turned off, and Carl reads the
words. The words are stored the same way as a typed question. To read an answer aloud, CarlNotes sends the text of that
answer to Google too. Google doesn't use either one to train its models. Google may log both for a limited time, only to
catch abuse and meet legal requirements.

## What Carl can change, and what he won't

If you can edit the topic, Carl can rewrite its prompt, add or remove a source, and change its settings. In the
new-topic chat, he makes a topic with you. He proposes each change first and saves it only after you say "yes". See
[Editing a topic with Coffee Talk](/docs/feed/editing-a-topic-in-chat/) and [Making a topic with Coffee
Talk](/docs/feed/making-a-topic-in-chat/).

## Getting better answers

- **Name the time window.** "Since Tuesday" beats "recently".
- **Ask for the disagreement, not the consensus.** The feed already shows the consensus.
- **Ask what's missing.** Carl's answer names the gaps in your source list and prompt.
