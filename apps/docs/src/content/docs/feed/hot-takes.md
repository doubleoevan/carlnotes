A hot take is what you say about something Carl found, and a reaction is one of six ways to respond to it with a
press. Both sit on every finding, podcast episode, and topic you can read, and every hot take takes reactions and
replies of its own.

The best hot takes go on the air. A topic's editors share them with Carl and Vienna, and the hosts of
[Coffee Break](/docs/feed/coffee-break/) tackle them in the topic's next podcast episode. See
[Hot takes for Carl and Vienna](#hot-takes-for-carl-and-vienna).

## React

You see six reactions: Like, Celebrate, Support, Love, Insightful, and Funny. Each shows how many people chose it. On
a topic's page and a podcast episode's page they sit beside the byline, and on a finding below its domain.

Hover over a reaction that has a number to see who chose it, newest first, with a link to each person's profile. In the
mobile app, press and hold it. The list names eight people and counts the rest.

Press one to react. Press it again to take it back, or press another to change it. You have one reaction on each
finding, podcast episode, topic, and hot take, and you can change it at any time.

Every reaction type is positive. To tell Carl a finding missed, a topic's owner and its followers use the thumbs down
on the finding, which only Carl sees.

## Leave a hot take

The **Hot takes** card sits below **Topic findings** on a topic's page and under the player on a podcast episode's
page. A finding has the same controls in its details. The heading shows how many hot takes and replies the last seven
days added.

![The Hot takes card on a topic page: the Hot Take button, 2 of 5 for Carl and Vienna at the top right, and a hot
take with its reactions, a reply, and a reply that mentions someone](../../../assets/screenshots/hot-takes-card.png)

1. Press **Hot Take**.
2. Write up to 2,000 characters. Line breaks stay.
3. To mention someone, type `@` and the start of their username, then pick them from the suggestions.
4. Press **Post**.

![The Add a hot take dialog: the text box, the Share with Carl and Vienna switch turned on, and
Post](../../../assets/screenshots/hot-take-composer.png)

Hot takes read newest first. Five show at first, the **more** link under the card shows the rest, and **show less**
returns to the first five. Each one has its reactions after its age and **Reply** under it. A reply answers the hot
take above it, and replies read oldest first under it, so a conversation stays in order. Replying to a reply joins the
same conversation.

On a podcast episode you can pick the chapter your hot take is about. The hot take then shows that chapter's tag, and
pressing the tag plays the podcast episode from there.

A hot take over 1,000 characters scrolls inside its own box, so one long post doesn't push the rest down the page.

You can edit a hot take for 15 minutes with the pencil. After that the pencil turns gray, and you can still delete it.

Text shows as plain text. A web address stays text and is not a link. A mention links to that person's profile.

## Hot takes for Carl and Vienna

Carl and Vienna, the hosts of Coffee Break, tackle hot takes in their podcast. They read each one out by its
writer's username and say what they make of it, the way radio hosts take a caller.

![Carl and Vienna in a podcast episode's transcript: Carl reads hotcup's hot take, Vienna answers it, and Carl reads
Bookish-Postscript's reply](../../../assets/screenshots/hot-takes-on-coffee-break.png)

A topic's owner and the members of the team that owns it can share a hot take with Carl and Vienna. Press the flame
beside a hot take to share it, and press it again to take it back. Everyone sees the lit flame while the hot take waits
for the next podcast episode, and two mugs once Carl and Vienna have talked about it.

A topic shares a few hot takes with each podcast episode: three on the Free plan and five on Plus and Premium. The
card's top right shows how many are waiting out of how many may be. A short podcast episode reads three and a full one
five, those with the most reactions first, and any left over wait for the next podcast episode. Once the podcast
episode's script has read them, the count starts again.

![The count at the Hot takes card's top right, 2 of 5 for Carl and Vienna, with its tooltip: Carl and Vienna like to
tackle hot takes in the podcast](../../../assets/screenshots/hot-takes-podcast-count.png)

When you write a hot take or a reply on a topic you edit, **Share with Carl and Vienna** is on below the text while the
topic has room for one. Turn it off to keep it to the people reading the topic. The same switch shows when you edit one,
and a hot take that Carl and Vienna have already talked about stays shared. If you can't edit the topic, editing your
own hot take while it waits takes it back until an editor shares it again.

What a shared hot take does:

- Carl and Vienna talk about it in the next podcast episode, by the username of whoever wrote it. A hot take on a
  finding the podcast episode covers comes up in that finding's chapter, and any other comes up just before the
  sign-off.
- Carl reads the most recently shared ones when he scores the topic's next findings.
- A web address in it goes into the topic's next scan, the way a link you send Carl does.
- It appears in the topic's sources list under **Hot takes**.

A topic's owner decides whether Carl and Vienna take hot takes at all, with the **Hot takes for Carl and Vienna**
switch under **Podcast** in **Edit topic**. It starts on. Turned off, the podcast leaves hot takes out,
and the shared ones still reach Carl's scoring and the topic's next scan.

![The Podcast settings in Edit topic: Coffee Break episode after every brew, and Hot takes for Carl and Vienna, both
on](../../../assets/screenshots/hot-takes-podcast-switch.png)

Apart from the check before it posts, Carl never reads a hot take that is not shared. A hot take and a reaction still
count as a signal when Carl ranks the topic's findings: a finding with a hot take stays in the topic when Carl trims the
topic to its best findings, and hot takes and reactions from established accounts make its page an example Carl reads
when he scores the next brew. Only the number counts, never the words.

## Who sees it

Hot takes and reactions are as visible as the topic they are on. On a public topic anyone can read them, signed in or
not. On a private or invite topic only the people who can open the topic read them.

You need an account to write. Anyone who can read a topic can react and leave a hot take there, unless the topic's
owner blocked them or they blocked the owner.

## Deleting and reporting

- You can delete your own hot take at any time. The trash can asks you to confirm, and a delete can't be undone.
  Deleting a hot take deletes its replies.
- A topic's owner can delete any hot take on their topic.
- Anyone signed in can report a finding, a podcast episode, or a hot take. Use **Report issue** beside it, or in the
  page's actions menu on a podcast episode. It stays on your screen with its flag filled in, and a person reviews the
  report within a day.

Blocking someone hides their hot takes and reactions from you and yours from them, and neither of you can post on the
other's topics. See [Reporting and blocking](/docs/account/reporting-and-blocking/).

Carl checks your text before it posts. If he declines it, he says so and keeps your draft.

## The daily email

The daily email is off until you turn it on. Once it is on, Carl emails you once a day with what happened since the
last email: hot takes and reactions on your topics, replies and reactions to your hot takes, and mentions of you. A day
with nothing sends nothing.

Turn it on or off with **Daily notifications email** under **Notifications** in Account. The link at the foot of any
of these emails also turns it off.

## From an AI app

A connected AI app can react, read a topic's hot takes, and leave a hot take or a reply for you. See
