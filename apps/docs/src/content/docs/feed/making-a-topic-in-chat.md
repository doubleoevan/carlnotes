You don't have to fill in the form. Tell Carl what you want to keep up with, and you build the topic together in your
words. The draft fills in below the conversation as you go. One yes creates the topic, with its first brew already
running.

![Carl suggesting sources in the new-topic chat, with the draft's title and prompt filled in on the card
below](../../../assets/screenshots/chat-new-topic-draft.png)

## Where it lives

Open the Coffee Talk panel and press the switcher under its title, which shows the current chat's name with a chevron.
Pick **Give Carl a topic. You know the one.** The option is there on every page for a signed-in user.

You can also start it from these places:

- Press **New Topic** on the home, activity, profile, or episode page, or on a topic page you can't brew, and pick
  **Build with Carl**.
- A page without that button has **New topic** in its actions menu, the **⋮** button beside the sort and filter icons.
  That option opens the same choice.
- If the home or profile page has no topics yet, it shows the same **Give Carl a topic. You know the one.** link, which
  opens this chat.
- On a team page, the **New topic** option at the end of the Add Topic picker does the same, with the team already on
  the draft.
- Ask for a new topic in any private chat, and Carl opens this one beside the chat you were in.

In the app, **New Topic** on the home page, **New topic** in the actions menu, and the **New topic** option at the end
of a team page's Add Topic picker offer the same two choices: **Do it myself** opens the topic form, and **Build with
Carl** opens this chat, with the team already on the draft from a team page. **Edit topic** offers **Edit with Carl**
the same way. The chat selector lists this chat after **Private chat**, and when Carl's answer links to it, the link
opens it. If your home page has no topics, the app opens this chat once each time you start it. The paperclip offers the
photo library, the camera, and Files, and each file waits on the draft card with an **X** to remove it until the topic
is made.

If you sign up with no topic yet, the panel opens on this chat by itself when you reach the home page. The message box
reads "Let's make your first topic. You know the one." If you close it, it stays closed until you come back.

## How the conversation goes

Carl takes it in order. Answer several questions at once, and he skips ahead. Change an answer, and he goes back and
rewrites that field.

1. **What to follow, and why.** One sentence is enough.
2. **A title and a prompt.** He proposes both in your words: what to look for, what to skip, and how fresh it has to be.
   Agree, and he writes them into the draft.
3. **Sources.** He suggests verified sources for that title and prompt, the same suggestions the editor's **Recommend**
   button makes, and asks which to keep. Carl and the **Recommend** button share a limit of 30 suggestion rounds a day.
   After that, he proposes sources from what he knows and says they are unverified.
4. **Who should see it.** Anyone, the people you invite, or only you. If you skip it, the topic is shared by invite.
5. **Which team.** A public or invite topic goes on a team you lead. He names your teams and asks which one, unless the
   draft already names one. With no team, or no team you lead, the topic is private.
6. **Who reads along.** Up to 20 email addresses, one per person. You can skip this.
7. **A file.** Attach a PDF, a spreadsheet, or notes with the paperclip. Carl reads it for that turn, and it waits in
   the draft. You can skip this.
8. **The settings.** Up to 10 tags, how often to brew (daily, weekdays, or weekly), the time and day it brews, and how
   many findings a brew keeps (5, 10, 15, or 20). Say what you want or skip it. The defaults are weekly and 10 findings.
   If you ask for daily and your plan has no daily topics left, the save says so and keeps the draft for another try.
9. **The read-back.** He reads the draft back and asks for a yes. Yes creates the topic.

## The draft card

Everything Carl has written down so far shows on the **Topic draft** card above the message box: the title, the prompt,
the tags, the schedule, how many findings a brew keeps, the visibility, the team, the invites, the sources with the
built-in web search first, and the files waiting. The card is what the yes saves. The files upload to the new topic
right after Carl creates it. Only the team can still fail at the save. If you no longer lead that team, Carl creates the
topic without it, and a toast says so. **Clear chat** clears the conversation and the draft together.

## After the yes

Carl creates the topic with what the draft holds. Anything you skipped takes the editor's defaults: shared by invite on
the team you picked, weekly on Wednesday at 9:00, and the top 10 findings per brew. The topic's page opens with **Carl
is Brewing…** already running. The people you named get their invites, and the files you attached upload to the topic as
attachments. The panel stays where it is, so you can start the next topic. You can change the schedule and everything
else on the topic page.

## When the plan is full

If your plan holds no more topics, the panel shows the limit in place of the draft, with a link to the plans page. Carl
says so before anything else. The free plan holds 3 topics, Plus holds 10, and Premium holds 25. Delete a topic from its
page, or move to a plan with more topics. See [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits/).
