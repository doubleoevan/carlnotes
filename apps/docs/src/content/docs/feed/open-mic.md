Open Mic is where the people behind a topic write in their own words. A post is long-form writing about the topic by
its owner or a member of a team that holds it. Carl draws its image, writes its summary, and records a short talk
about it with Vienna. Everyone who can read the topic can read its posts, and anyone signed in can react and leave a
hot take.

## Write a post

1. Open a topic you own, or one a team of yours holds, and find the **Open Mic** section.
2. Choose **Post**. Your draft opens in the editor, and only you can see it.
3. Give it a title and write. The editor saves as you go and shows your word count.
4. Choose **Publish** once the post has a title and 300 words.

Carl checks the text before it goes out. If he declines it, he says so and keeps your draft.

You can edit a post for 15 minutes after it goes out. After that the text stays as it is. You can delete your post at
any time, and so can the topic's owner. Deleting a post removes its hot takes, its reactions, and its recording.

## What a post's page shows

- **The image.** Carl draws one from the post when it publishes. Press the image to regenerate it, with an optional
  note on what to draw, or to upload your own. The post's author and the topic's editors can change it. Regenerations
  count toward the same monthly limit as topic images.
- **The summary.** Press the info button beside the title for Carl's summary. An edit inside the 15 minutes writes it
  again.
- **Reactions and hot takes.** They work as they do everywhere else. See [Hot takes](/docs/feed/hot-takes/). A hot
  take on a post is never shared with Carl and Vienna until one of the topic's editors shares it.
- **Report image.** The page's menu reports the image. You stop seeing an image you reported until it changes.

A post on a private or invite topic is as private as the topic. A public topic's post has its own link to share.

- **Share.** Press Share in the post's menu, or in the summary the info button opens, for the same options a topic
  has. A shared link shows the post's own card: its image, its title, its author, and its topic. A private or invite
  topic's post shows the topic's image and owner with the post's title.
- **Invite to a post.** On an invite topic, the owner's invite link from a post follows the topic for whoever accepts
  it, then opens the post.

## The Coffee Break Discussion

Carl and Vienna record a Coffee Break Discussion about each post: two to seven minutes of the two of them discussing
it amongst themselves. A longer post gets a longer discussion, and so does a post with hot takes: they read up to six
of the hot takes that were shared with them and credit each writer by username.

The first recording starts 15 minutes after the post goes out, once its text can no longer change. Press play on the
post's page to listen. It plays in the same player as the [Coffee Break podcast](/docs/feed/coffee-break/), so it
keeps playing as you move around.

If you can publish on the topic, **Regenerate** records it again. You can add a discussion prompt on what Carl and
Vienna should talk about. The recording you had keeps playing until the new one is ready. Regenerations have a
monthly limit by
plan, shown beside the button. See [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits/). A recording uses the Coffee
fund of whoever started it: the author for the first, and whoever pressed Regenerate after that.

## Ask Carl about a post

Open [Coffee Talk](/docs/feed/chat/) from the pill at the bottom right of a post's page. It opens straight on your
private chat about that post, and the question box reads **Ask me for a hot take on this post**. Carl answers from the
post and from the hot takes readers shared, and he credits a hot take to the person who wrote it. The conversation is
private to you. It is saved in the topic's private chat, where each question shows a tag with the post's name. A
visitor is asked to sign up first, and comes back to the post.

Carl changes nothing from a post's page. To edit the topic, ask him from the topic's own page.

## Where posts show up

- The **Open Mic** section of the topic's page, newest first. Your own drafts come first, and only you see them.
- **Featured Posts** on the author's Café, right below Featured Podcasts. It lists every post the author published.
- **Featured Posts** on the page of a team that holds the topic, and **Featured posts** on the home page. See
  [Featuring a post](#featuring-a-post).
- **New posts this week** on the home page, right below Featured posts: every post published on a public topic in the
  last seven days, newest first. The section shows only in a week that has a new post.
- **Brews News**, when a topic you own, follow, or have through a team gets a new post.
- **Search**, by a published post's title, its summary, or its text. A post on a private or invite topic shows only to
  the people who can open the topic. **Posts**, under **Search for** in Filters, turns them on or off. See
  [Searching](/docs/feed/searching/).

Carl also reads a topic's published posts as context when he reviews findings in its next brew.

## Featuring a post

Featured Posts shows only when it holds a post, and it pages six cards at a time.

- **Your Café.** The section lists every post you published. Open the actions menu on your own Café and choose
  **Featured posts**. Add a post, move the rows into the order you want, and press **Save**. You can feature as many
  of your published posts on public topics as you like. Your featured posts lead the section in your order. After
  them come all your other posts, newest first. A visitor sees your posts on public topics only.
- **A post's own page.** Open the actions menu on one of your published posts on a public topic and choose
  **Featured posts**. The same list opens with that post added at the end. Press a row to move the post there, then
  press **Save**.
- **A team's page.** The section lists every post published on the team's topics. A team has no list of its own.
  Posts that their authors featured on their Cafés lead the section, and all the other posts follow, newest first.
  Someone who is not on the team sees the posts on its public topics only.
- **The home page.** The section shows up to 12 posts from public topics, one per topic: the ones an admin featured
  first, then the newest. An admin features a post from **Home posts**, in the actions menu of the home page or of
  the post's own page, on the web.

A featured post leaves the list when it is deleted, or when its topic stops being public. A post on a private or
invite topic shows only to the people who can open the topic, and nobody else sees its card.

## From an AI app

A connected AI app can find posts with its search, list a topic's posts, read one, read its hot takes, and leave yours.
It cannot write or publish a post. See [Add CarlNotes to your AI app](/docs/account/add-to-ai/).
