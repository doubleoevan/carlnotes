The search bar at the top of every page finds topics, podcast episodes, findings, teams, and profiles by name. It finds
what you can open: public results for everyone, and your own topics, your teams' topics, and the invite topics you
follow once you sign in.

## Typing

Type two or more characters and the bar lists its first 10 results as you type. Press **Enter** to open the only result,
or the one you highlighted with the arrow keys. With more than one result, Enter opens the search page.

## The search page

The search page lists every result for your search, numbered, 10 to a page. The title says how many it found, such as
"14 search results for "speed"". The numbered pages under the list open the rest, and a result keeps its number across
pages.

![The search page for "speed": the Tags picker and New Topic button, the title "14 search results for "speed"", and
numbered rows for a topic, two podcast episodes, and findings, each finding with its chapter's play
pill](../../../assets/screenshots/search-results.png)

Each row names its kind:

- **Topic**: its viewers and followers, with its latest podcast episode's play pill.
- **Podcast**: the topic it belongs to, how long ago it was published, and its listeners.
- **Finding**: its source and age, the play pill of the chapter that narrates it, and its topic.
- **Team**: how many topics and members it has.
- **Profile**: a person's public profile.

Click a finding to open its note, the same note the feed shows: Carl's notes, the link to the page, and the rating,
bookmark, and read controls the topic allows you. A bookmarked finding keeps its number and shows its bookmark after its
age.

## Narrowing a search

The search page's address holds the whole search, so a copied link opens the same results for someone else.

- **Filters** turns each kind of result on or off under **Search for**. Its **Read**, **Watch**, and **Listen** rows
  narrow findings by kind.
- **Unread** and **Bookmarked**, in Filters, show findings alone, since only a finding can be read or bookmarked.
- **Tags**, on the left above the results, keeps the topics with those tags and what belongs to them. **Filters** sets
  the match: **Any Match**, **All Match**, **Exclude Tags**, or **Off**. Teams and profiles have no tags, so Any Match
  and All Match leave them out.
- **Sort** orders by **Relevance**, **Newest**, or **Trending**. Relevance puts exact name matches first. Trending puts
  the most viewed topics, the most played podcast episodes, and the most opened findings first.

## Going home

Click the CarlNotes logo on the search page to clear the search bar and go home. On any other page, while the bar still
holds your search, the logo opens that search again.
