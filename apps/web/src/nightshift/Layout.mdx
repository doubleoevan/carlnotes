# Layout

This page states the section order rules that every page follows, on the web and in the app.

## Films come right before podcasts

A podcast episode can have a film. Wherever a page shows films and podcasts, the film section sits right before the
podcast section it pairs with, so the newer format leads. Four pages pair them today, six times:

- Home: Featured films, then Featured podcasts
- Home: New films this week, then New podcasts this week
- Café: Featured Films, then Featured Podcasts
- Team: Featured Films, then Featured Podcasts
- Topic: Coffee Break Cinema, then the podcast player
- Topic: Coffee Break films, then Coffee Break podcast episodes

The podcast episode page is the exception: Coffee Break podcast, with the audio player, comes first, and Coffee Break
Cinema, with the film, follows it. `sectionOrder.test.ts` checks that order on both apps too.

The home's order lives in `HOME_SECTIONS` in `packages/core/lib/homeFaces.ts`, which both apps render in order. The
Café's lives in `PROFILE_SECTIONS` in `packages/core/lib/profileSections.ts` and in each app's profile page. The
team page's lives in `TEAM_SECTIONS` in `packages/core/lib/cardSections.ts` and in each app's team page. The
topic page's order lives in each app's topic page. `packages/core/lib/sectionOrder.test.ts` fails if any pair loses
its order. A new page that shows films and podcasts adds its pair to that test.

A film section with no film is hidden, so a page without films reads as it did before.

## The home's sections

The home shows its card sections in one order: Featured topics, Featured films, New films this week, Featured
podcasts, New podcasts this week, Featured posts, New posts this week, and Your topics for a user. Each week's new
cards sit right after their featured cards. A "this week" section lists the last seven days alone, so it is absent in
a week with nothing new. `sectionOrder.test.ts` fails if `HOME_SECTIONS` or either app's home leaves that order.

## The Café's Topic Posts

The Café has one Topic Posts section, Featured Posts, the last of its card sections. It lists every post the person
published that the reader may open: the pinned ones in their order, then the rest newest first. Open Mic names the
section of a topic's page alone.

## The team page's sections

A team page has the Café's four card sections, under the same titles in the same order: Featured Topics, Featured
Films, Featured Podcasts, and Featured Posts. A team has no pins of its own. The cards are those of its topics that
the reader may open, and the pins their owners and authors set on their Cafés lead each section. Featured Posts lists
every published post of those topics, the pinned ones first and then the rest newest first.
`sectionOrder.test.ts` fails if `TEAM_SECTIONS` or either app's team page leaves the Café's titles or that order.
