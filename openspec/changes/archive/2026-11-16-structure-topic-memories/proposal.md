## Why

Carl's Journal can lose distinct facts when a similarity decision combines them in storage. The independent judge also disagrees with human readers often enough to limit what its score can establish.

## What Changes

- Give each atomic Topic Memory a normalized subject, fixed claim type, value, and event date at its known precision.
- Combine exact same-value facts across compatible dates. Keep the original row and its evidence in recoverable history. A model-confirmed update to a single-valued fact moves the predecessor and its feedback to history.
- Group similar claims with equal values for Journal and prompt reads. Include one ranked claim per group in a prompt.
- Give the dev owner a blinded phone review with separate human and judge scores.
- Align the wrapped Journal control on narrow web screens and use names in standalone claims.
- Keep the production Journal button disabled by default until measured displayed redundancy drops below 10%.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `topic-memories` defines structured writes, reversible reads, and evidence for the human evaluation.

## Impact

The change touches the Journal storage and read contracts, Scan activities, model templates, Nightshift row, and dev repair and evaluation commands.
