## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: A public topic page links more topics

**Reason**: The "Similar topics" table at the end of the same page lists up to fifty server-rendered links to the
public shown Topics most related to it, ranked by meaning and shared tags, so the five "More topics" links repeated it
with a weaker order. A signed-in user's table also lists the Topics they may open through their account.

**Migration**: None. A crawler and a visitor without JavaScript follow the "Similar topics" links instead.
