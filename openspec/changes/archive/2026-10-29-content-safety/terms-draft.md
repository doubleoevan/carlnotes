# Terms wording draft, for human review

This is the draft of the Terms section this change adds, and the lines it changes. Nothing here is published until
the owner approves the wording. The published Terms page is `apps/web/src/pages/TermsPage.tsx`, and the draft follows
its existing section numbering, so the new section is 6a, between Acceptable use and Plans.

## Changes to section 6, Acceptable use

Add two bullets to the "Do not" list:

- Post a message, note, or comment that harasses, threatens, or demeans another person, or that is sexual, violent,
  hateful, or otherwise objectionable
- Post sexual content involving minors, or any other content that exploits or endangers a child. We remove it, and
  we report it to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children or the equivalent authority

## New section 6a, Objectionable content, reports, and removals

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people. Messages, notes, comments, topic names and prompts,
team names, and usernames are read by other people, so each one is checked before it posts, and a post the check
rejects is not published. A check is not a guarantee, so anyone can report content or a person from the page where
they see it, and can block a person so that person's messages, comments, and invitations no longer reach them.

We read every report. We act on a report within one day of receiving it: we remove content that breaks these rules,
and we may suspend or close the account that posted it. Content we remove is removed for everyone. Removal and
suspension are at our discretion, and we tell the person whose content was removed where that is practical.

To report content outside the app, or to reach us about a removal, email support@carlnotes.com.

## The Contact section

Add a line above the existing address:

Questions about these terms, a report, or a removal go to support@carlnotes.com.

support@carlnotes.com is the one published address, here and on the Privacy page. The owner approved this wording on
2026-10-29.

## Terms version

The published Terms will state a version date on their first line, the date this section is published. The app
records that date on every account that accepts the Terms, and asks an existing account to accept once after the
date changes. Changing the date is the only step that asks every account again, so wording fixes that do not change
what a person agrees to keep the date.
