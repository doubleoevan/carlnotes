# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A plan card states its image regenerations a month

Each pricing card SHALL list one line stating how many topic image regenerations the plan allows a month, 2 on free,
15 on plus, and 30 on premium, and the plans documentation SHALL carry the same row. A user's monthly spend SHALL
include their recorded topic image render costs.

#### Scenario: The cards show the limit

- **WHEN** a reader opens the plans page
- **THEN** the free card reads 2 image regenerations a month and the premium card reads 30
