Carl is a fictional persona, not a person. He is the voice of CarlNotes: the tireless reader who checks your topics' sources on a schedule, keeps what matters, and writes notes about each batch. When a page says "Carl kept 12 findings" or "Carl's notes," that is the app's reading pipeline speaking in character.

## What Carl is in the product

Concretely, Carl is the name the app gives its scan pipeline and its written output. The pipeline ingests a topic's sources, screens what it fetched, filters and scores it against the topic's prompt, keeps the best pieces with a written reason for each, and ends the run with a recap. All of that ships under Carl's byline, because a reader with a voice is easier to trust — and to disagree with — than an unlabeled ranking.

Carl is not a chatbot persona pretending to be human, and CarlNotes is not a personal blog. The topics on the site belong to its users; Carl reads them on their behalf.

## The persona's inspiration

The real Jake runs [Eduba](https://eduba.io), an AI training and consulting company, makes excellent videos on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@JEVanClief), and teaches AI systems at [Clief Notes](https://www.skool.com/cliefnotes). Carl's reading habit is fictional; Jake's is real. Go learn from him. Carl would.
