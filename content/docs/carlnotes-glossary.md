title: Topics, Brews, and Findings — the CarlNotes glossary
description: What the words on a CarlNotes page mean, from Topic and Source to Brew, Finding, and the brew diary.
date: 2026-08-12
---

CarlNotes has its own small vocabulary, poured mostly from a coffee pot. Here is what each word means, so any page on the site reads without guessing.

**Topic** — a standing question you give Carl: a name, a prompt in your own words, and the sources to read. Everything else hangs off a topic.

**Source** — one place a topic reads: the built-in web search, an RSS feed, a subreddit, or a YouTube channel.

**Scan, or Brew** — one run of a topic through its sources. The app's code says scan; the pages say brew, because Carl runs on coffee. Daily topics brew daily, weekly topics weekly, and an owner can brew one by hand.

**Finding** — one piece a brew kept: a link with a title, a relevance score, and a short explanation of why Carl kept it. Findings are ranked, and the explanation ships with each one.

**Resource** — the page, video, or episode behind a finding. A finding is Carl's judgment about a resource.

**Brew diary** — the topic's scan history: one entry per brew with what was read, what was kept, how long it took, what it cost, and Carl's notes on the batch.

**Carl's notes, or the recap** — the paragraph Carl writes about each brew's batch, citing the findings it kept.

**Kept / reviewed** — the two numbers on a profile's topic table: how many findings a topic holds against how many resources its brews looked at.

**Feed** — the home page's stream of topics and their findings. A topic's feed is built from its findings at read time; there is no separate feed to maintain.

**Follow, or Subscription** — following a topic delivers its brews to you, optionally by email. The pages say follow; the code says subscription.

**Visibility** — who may see a topic: public (anyone), invite (people the owner invited by email), or private (the owner alone).

**Invite topic** — a topic shared with a list. An invitee can see the topic once signed in with the invited address, and findings start arriving from the first brew after they follow.

**Plans** — how much coffee you buy Carl: how many topics, how many daily brews, and the monthly reading budget.

**And Carl himself** — the persona who does the reading. He drinks coffee, reads everything, and has notes.
