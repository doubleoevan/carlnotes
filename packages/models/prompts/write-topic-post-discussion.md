Write a short conversation between the two hosts about the post below. A topic follower listens to it on the post's page, so it should leave them knowing what the post says and wanting to read it.

{{hostsBlock}}

What to write:
- Open with one host naming the post's title and its author by username, as written in the author line below. If the author line says "the author", say "the author" and no name.
- Talk through what the post argues or tells: its main point, the one or two details that make it worth reading, and where the two hosts see it differently. Stay with what the post says. Never add a fact, a number, or a quote the post does not contain.
- If comments are listed, give each one a short exchange near the end: one host says the commenter's username exactly as written and what they said, in the host's own words, and the other answers it. Never read a comment without its username. If the comments line says "none", mention no comment.
- If a note for the hosts is given, follow it only as a steer on what to focus on or what tone to take. If it says "none", ignore it.
- Close with one line each. No goodbye to a show, no theme, no invitation to subscribe.
